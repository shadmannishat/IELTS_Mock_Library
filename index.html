How it works

Test day, in your browser

Each mock follows the real computer-delivered IELTS Academic order and timing. Mocks 1–28 run fully offline from this folder. Mocks 29 and up stream their Listening audio (and some Writing charts) from the internet, so they are marked “needs internet”. Nothing you write is uploaded anywhere.

Taking a mock

  1. Enter your Student ID (numbers only) and, if you like, your name.
  2. Pick a mock and run the quick ready-check: sound test, headphones, quiet room.
  3. The mock opens with your ID filled in. Press Begin Full Mock, then each section's Start Test button.
  4. When you finish Writing, you see your bands and the answer review. Press Back to Mock Library to save them here; you can reopen the review from Progress.

Rules inside each section

  • Security mode is off by default, so the test won't lock to fullscreen. Turn it on in the ready check (or on the mock's start screen) when the computer is online: then leaving fullscreen or switching tabs is a violation, and the third one submits the section.
  • Answers and scores stay hidden until the whole mock is finished. Then you see your bands and a full answer review.
  • You enter your Student ID once. Every section, including Writing, uses it automatically.
  • Spellcheck, red underlines and word suggestions are switched off, as in the real test.
  • The Listening recording plays once only. You can't pause or rewind it.
  • You can't go back to a section after submitting it.
  • Your Writing answers download as a .txt file named with your Student ID. Hand it to your teacher.

Timing

  • Listening: about 30 minutes (4 parts, 40 questions), including 2 minutes to check answers.
  • Reading: 60 minutes (3 passages, 40 questions).
  • Writing: 60 minutes (Task 1: 150+ words, Task 2: 250+ words).
  • Allow about 2 hours 40 minutes in total, with no break between sections.

For teachers

  • Keep this whole folder together: index.html and the mocks folder. Open index.html in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  • Results are stored in this browser on this computer. Use Progress → Export CSV to collect them; tick "Show all students" to export everyone.
  • To run it on several computers, copy the folder to each one, or upload the folder to any static web host and share the link.
  • Each mock can also be opened on its own from the mocks folder.
