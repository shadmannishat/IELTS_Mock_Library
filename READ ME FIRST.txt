IELTS 7+ MOCK LIBRARY
Md Salim Shadman, Head of IELTS and Language Teaching

HOW TO START
1. Keep this folder together: index.html and the "mocks" folder must stay side by side.
2. Double-click index.html. Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
3. Enter the Student ID, choose a mock, finish the ready check, then press Start.

WHAT HAPPENS
- The Student ID is entered once. Listening, Reading and Writing all use it automatically.
- Security mode (fullscreen lock) is OFF by default. Switch it on in the ready check, or on the
  mock's start screen, when the computer is connected to the internet.
- Answers and scores are hidden until the whole mock is finished. Then the student sees the bands
  and a full answer review (their answer next to the correct answer).
- Spellcheck, red underlines and word suggestions are switched off in every section.
- After the Writing section, press "Back to Mock Library". The bands are saved under Progress,
  and the answer review can be reopened there with "Review answers".
- Writing answers download as a .txt file named with the Student ID. Hand it to the teacher for marking.

FOR TEACHERS
- Results are saved in this browser on this computer. Open Progress > Export CSV to collect them.
  Tick "Show all students on this computer" to export everyone.
- To use more computers, copy the whole folder to each one, or upload the folder to any static web host.
- Everything works offline. Nothing is sent over the internet.
